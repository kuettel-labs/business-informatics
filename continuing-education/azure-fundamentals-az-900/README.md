**Goal:** pass Microsoft Azure Fundamentals (AZ-900).

> **How to use this**

## Exam facts

- Passing score: 700 (scale 1-1000). Roughly 40-60 questions, about 45 minutes of answer time.
- Question styles: multiple choice, yes/no series (each statement judged separately), drag-and-drop matching, hotspot/sentence completion.
- Free [Microsoft Learn Practice Assessment for AZ-900](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/azure-fundamentals/practice/assessment?assessment-type=practice&assessmentId=23).
- [Exam sandbox](https://aka.ms/examdemo) to try the exam UI.

## Exam-day logistics

- **ID:** valid, unexpired government-issued photo ID; the name must match the certification profile exactly.
- **Check-in:** online-proctored check-in opens 30 minutes before the start; start it early. You photograph your ID, your face and the four sides of the room. Run the system test on the same computer and network beforehand.
- **Room rules (online proctored):** private, quiet room, door closed, nobody entering. Clear desk; no paper, pens, notes, watches, second monitor or headset. Phone out of arm's reach (you may be asked to show it). Stay in camera view, do not read questions aloud, no food; covering your mouth or looking away repeatedly can be flagged. Breaks: unscheduled breaks are allowed but the exam clock keeps running and you cannot return to earlier questions; see the current [Microsoft exam break policy](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/support/exam-duration-exam-experience#unscheduled-breaks-on-exams).
- **Test centre alternative:** arrive 15 minutes early with ID; personal items go into a locker.
- Current rules: [Pearson VUE online exam requirements](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/online-exams).
